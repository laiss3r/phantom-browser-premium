Phantom optional Free Tor routing

Tor is an independent project, not an endorsement of Phantom. Phantom is not
Tor Browser. Stock Chromium over SOCKS does not provide Tor Browser's privacy
properties and may expose identifying information. Shared Tor exits may be
slow, blocked or unavailable in the selected country. No anonymity, no-leak,
residential-IP, dedicated-IP or guaranteed-country service is promised.

On first use the application downloads the official unmodified Tor expert
bundle 15.0.26 (Tor 0.4.9.14) directly from https://dist.torproject.org/.
Windows x64: about 22 MiB; Linux x64: about 31 MiB. No account/API key is used.
The installer does not redistribute Tor executable binaries. Archive checksums
and extracted-file hashes are pinned in the app; modified files are rejected.
The original license/component notices are retained in network-runtime/docs.
Tor source: https://dist.torproject.org/tor-0.4.9.14.tar.gz
Expert bundle/build information: https://download.torproject.org/tor/
Upstream licenses and source terms apply to the separately downloaded software.

Keyless IP/country lookup: https://country.is/ (https://api.country.is/).
This external service receives explicit lookup requests through the assigned
proxy. IP location is approximate, not GPS. Rate limits/outages can cause a
truthful failed/unavailable connection result. No service credentials are
embedded in this product. The automatic Tor download reaches Tor Project over
your normal connection; Tor network usage may be visible to your ISP.
